# BIBLE + OUTLINE
Build the immutable story bible first, then the outline.

Bible must define:
genre promise, theme, world rules, protagonist wound/desire/need, antagonist pressure,
major character voices, relationship map, timeline constraints, prohibited contradictions.

Outline must define:
opening hook, inciting incident, escalating nodes, midpoint shift, crisis, climax, ending,
chapter-level goal/conflict/change/hook and planned foreshadowing/payoffs.
Do not write prose before a chapter outline exists.
